    Missable audit — why the count is 1, not 10

    Every trophy was checked against its official description and the launch guides. Only Positive Mental Attitude can be lost for good: Lucia’s Chapter 1 balcony meditation never comes back. The candidates that look missable are not: Kenton vanishes for a chapter but returns (call Brian as Jason in Chapter 2 to speed it up); Andy, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret all stay available in free-roam after the story; the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands persist; Kingpin Bowling’s robbery icon respawns if you miss a valuable; and optional missions that disappear never block a trophy or the 100% checklist. Sources: PowerPyx, GTABase, EGamers.

    Do these early — the only time-gated steps

    PowerPyx’s launch guide lists exactly one potentially missable trophy. Three more steps are gated behind story chapters, so do them when they appear. Everything else can be cleaned up in free-roam after the story.
